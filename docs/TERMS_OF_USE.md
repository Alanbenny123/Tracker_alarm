# Destination Alarm terms of use

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Destination Alarm helps you set local time or distance alerts for chosen destinations. Location accuracy, Android power settings, device availability, and signal conditions can delay or prevent an alert. Metro tunnels and other indoor areas may have no usable GPS signal. Check your route and surroundings yourself; do not rely on this app as your only way to know when to get off or arrive.

Map and place information is supplied by Google Maps Platform and is subject to the [Google Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms), [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), and applicable [Google Maps Platform terms](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms). Do not use the app to copy or redistribute map tiles or place information. The app requires internet access to load maps, resolve shortened Google Maps links, and search for places; monitoring an already-set destination can use offline GPS.

You control the destinations, alert radii, repeat days, and Android permissions you choose. Alerts are approximate because GPS can be inaccurate and straight-line distance may differ from travel distance. You can end an active alarm at any time.

The app is provided without a guarantee that an alert will arrive at an exact time or location. For questions about these terms, open an issue at [the Destination Alarm project](https://github.com/Alanbenny123/Tracker_alarm/issues).
