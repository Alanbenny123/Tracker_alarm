# Destination Alarm privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Destination Alarm uses your location to show your position, find nearby destinations, and ring alarms when you approach a destination. With an alarm active, the app accesses location while it is in the background or your screen is locked. It runs an Android foreground service and displays a persistent monitoring notification. You can stop an alarm in the app or from its notification, and you can revoke location access in Android settings.

## Information kept on your device

The app stores active destination alarms, selected destinations, saved places, settings, and up to 100 recent trip records on your device. This includes destination names and coordinates, alert radii, repeat days, and alarm status. The app has no account or developer-operated server. Android cloud backup and device-transfer extraction are disabled for this app. You can delete saved places, trip history, and damaged recovery copies in Settings; ending an alarm removes it from active monitoring. Uninstalling the app removes its local data, subject to copies made by your device or by an older app version.

## Google services

The standard 2D map uses Google Maps SDK for Android. Place-name searches and nearby bus, metro, and railway searches use Google Places SDK for Android. Google receives map requests, search text, and the searched area's coordinates; Google may also receive technical information such as your IP address and device data needed to provide these services. Maps and search require an internet connection. An alarm already set for a destination checks its saved coordinates using Android location and can work without map tiles or internet access. Google processes data under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms).

If you share a Google Maps link to the app, it connects to Google to resolve a shortened link. If the normal connection fails, Google Public DNS over HTTPS may receive the short-link hostname to help resolve it. The app does not send your saved alarm list or trip history to a developer-operated server.

## Notifications and sound

The app uses notifications, vibration, alarm audio, and lock-screen alarm access to alert you. Android may restrict any of these permissions or background activity. You can change them in Android settings.

## Ads and changes

This version does not display ads or use an advertising SDK. If ads or analytics are added, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration must be updated before that version is released.

## Contact

For privacy questions or deletion help, open an issue at [the Destination Alarm project](https://github.com/Alanbenny123/Tracker_alarm/issues). You can also delete the app's local data through Settings or Android's app storage controls.
